Product Images & Media Guide

Step-by-step instructions to upload high-quality images and videos for products.

1. FreeImage.host (Recommended Free Host)

  1. Go to FreeImage.host.
  2. Upload your photo and click Upload.
  3. Select "HTML Full Linked" or copy the Direct Image Link (ending in .jpg / .png).
  4. Paste the link in the Image URL field.

2. YouTube & Instagram Reels

  1. Upload demo/unboxing video on YouTube or Instagram.
  2. Copy the browser URL of your video.
  3. Paste it into the YouTube / Video URL field in Single Add / Edit modal.

Store Owner Guidelines for Adding Products
  • Product Name: Enter the complete brand and product title (e.g. Fortune Sunlite Sunflower Oil).
  • Selling Price vs MRP: Selling Price is what the customer pays; MRP displays with a strikethrough showing savings.
  • Unit / Packaging: Specify quantity (e.g. 1 Litre, 5 kg Pack, 500g Jar).
  • Image URL: Click Upload to pick an image or paste a link from FreeImage.host.
