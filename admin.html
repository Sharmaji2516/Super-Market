ADMIN PANEL

Admin Quick Guide

1. Product Images

Method A (Postimages)
  • Step 1 Go to Postimages.org.
  • Step 2 Upload your photo and copy the "Direct Link".
  • Step 3 Paste it into the Image URL field below.
Method B (FreeImage.host)
  • Step 1 Go to FreeImage.host.
  • Step 2 Click to browse and upload the image from your computer.
  • Step 3 Once uploaded, right-click the image.
  • Step 4 Once the image opens in a new tab, left-click on the image and select "Copy image address" (or "Copy image link").
  • Step 5 Return to the Admin Dashboard and paste the link into the Image URL field below.

2. Product Videos

YouTube Upload
  • Step 1 Upload your video to YouTube (set as "Unlisted" if preferred).
  • Step 2 Copy the video URL from your browser address bar.
  • Step 3 Paste it into the Video URL field below.
